## Chapter 13 · Cancer overview

:::qa
Cancer is the ___ most common cause of death in the US. ⟶ **B. Second.**
Benign vs malignant cells? ⟶ **C.** Malignant cells lack contact inhibition.
Key lab for cancer with bone metastasis? ⟶ **B. Calcium** (hypercalcemia).
Most specific way to diagnose malignancy? ⟶ **D. Biopsy.**
Chemo before definitive surgery is? ⟶ **C. Neoadjuvant.**
Which statement shows chemo teaching worked? ⟶ **B.** "It is important that I monitor my temperature."
Mrs. Jones, stage III? ⟶ **C.** Large, locally advanced, possibly in local nodes. (Class notes said D, "spread to lymph and blood systems." The book says C. Confirm with your instructor.)
Oral hormonal therapy after mastectomy? ⟶ **B. Adjuvant.**
Call the provider immediately for? ⟶ **C. Fever** (myelosuppression).
Before first paclitaxel (SATA)? ⟶ **B, D** (and **C** per class): emergency equipment ready (hypersensitivity), check IV blood return (irritant/vesicant), and ECG within 24 hours (class adds this; paclitaxel can affect heart rhythm). Go with B, C, D.
Left chest wall radiation, which systems (SATA)? ⟶ **B, D, E** (and **C** per class): integumentary (radiodermatitis), cardiac (late cardiac risk), respiratory (pneumonitis), plus musculoskeletal (shoulder stiffness and fibrosis). Go with B, C, D, E.
Class PLQ 1: Regulated cell death that cancer cells lose is called? ⟶ **C. Apoptosis.**
Class PLQ 2: Stage III T3, N2, M1: which statement needs more teaching? ⟶ **A.** "I am relieved that the cancer is still local." M1 = metastasis present, so it is not local.
Class PLQ 3: Leukemia patient with chemo neutropenia: include in the plan? ⟶ **C.** Restricting visitors with communicable diseases.
:::

## Chapters 18–19 · Immune

:::qa
Central lymphoid organs? ⟶ **D. Thymus and bone marrow.**
Needs more bone marrow biopsy teaching? ⟶ **C.** "Odds are I will die from this procedure."

Class PLQ 1: Expected action of an immunoglobulin infusion? ⟶ **D.** They enhance the recognition of antigens.

Class PLQ 3: Primary immune deficiency warning signs (SATA)? ⟶ **D, E, F:** frequent, severe infections needing hospitalization · persistent lymphadenopathy · failure to thrive.
Class PLQ 4: CATCH (DiGeorge) components (SATA)? ⟶ **D, E, F, G, H:** cardiac defects · abnormal facies · thymic hypoplasia · cleft palate · hypocalcemia.
