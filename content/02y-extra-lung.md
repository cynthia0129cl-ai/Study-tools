## More from your first study guide
- **Memory:** small cell = **S**peedy, **S**preads, **S**ad survival. NSCLC types = "All Smokers Lose" (**A**deno, **S**quamous, **L**arge).
- **Headache = brain mets, the #1 site.** Bone pain (bone mets) → check calcium. Lung cancer can also cause SIADH, hypercalcemia, and **SVC syndrome.** "New cough that doesn't go away" is the **N** in CAUTION.
- Lung cancer risk factors also include age >65, air pollution, coal/soot/tar, wood dust.
- **Slide wording:** the nursing diagnoses are *ineffective gas exchange related to disruption of pulmonary cells*, *anxiety related to inability to breathe effectively*, and *activity intolerance related to fatigue and dyspnea*.
- **Day 1:** palliative care starts upon diagnosis (Connection Check 26.4). It's **not the same as hospice.**
- Asthma case reminder: a quiet chest (no wheezes + diminished breath sounds) = obstruction/emergency → **airway first.** Controller meds daily even when well; don't stop prednisone suddenly.
- **Only biopsy confirms** lung cancer.
