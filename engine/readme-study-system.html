Read me

The same page is in the drill and in the Mastery Study Guide. They are built from the same facts and always agree.

How to use the drill

  1. Start with the pre-test. It asks a few questions from every topic (none from the last pass) and shows your level in each: Strong, Okay or Weak.
  2. Do your daily sessions. Pick topics, a length (5 to 500, endless, or a timed 30 or 45 minute plan) and tap Start. Leave Think first on: try to recall the answer before the choices appear.
  3. Check each answer. Read “Why my pick is wrong”, then the key idea. After a right answer tap Knew it or I guessed. A guess comes back sooner.
  4. Star anything you want to see again. Use the filters (missed, starred, last pass, never practiced) to drill just those.
  5. Fix my misses shows the mistakes you keep making and your shaky questions by concept. “Fix this” re-teaches the concept and asks you a different question on it.
  6. Last pass. Use it in the final days. It asks every last-pass question once, shakiest first. “Just the 20 weakest” is the short version.
  7. A question is solid only after you get the whole question right on two spaced visits (three for true/false). Your progress is saved on this device and, when you open the page as a claude.ai artifact, in your account so your phone and laptop match.

How to use the guide

  1. The guide teaches the same facts in lecture order. Each section gives the mechanism first, then the priority actions, a patient example, the common trap and a memory trick.
  2. Tap each blank to reveal it, but say the answer out loud first.
  3. The Key ideas box at the end of each section is the short version to re-read the night before.

How to use both together

  • Drill to find and prove. The drill finds what you do not know yet and proves what you do.
  • Guide to look up and re-learn. When a card in Fix my misses shows a concept, tap its link to open the same section in the guide. Read that section, then go back and prove it in the drill.
  • Every fact on a Fix my misses card is in the guide section it links to.
